Gang pay calculator

Convert a reviewed takeoff and rate card into a transparent stage-by-stage gang-pay estimate.

What this prototype does

The calculator shows exactly how each lift, birdcage, access item and addition contributes to the gang’s draft payment, replacing an opaque spreadsheet total with reviewable formulas.

Used by
Contracts managers, commercial teams and payroll reviewers
Starts with
Confirmed takeoff quantities, included work and an editable gang rate card
Produces
A stage-by-stage gang-pay estimate and copyable payment summary
Try it: Toggle an included item, change a quantity or rate, and see the relevant stage and total recalculate.This is a calculation prototype, not payroll. The original worked-example rates remain editable and require commercial confirmation before operational use.
