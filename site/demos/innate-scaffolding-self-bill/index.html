Client pricing matrix

Hold agreed plot values, extras and hire in one editable client pricing schedule.

What this prototype does

The matrix organises client-agreed scaffold values by plot and stage, calculates payment-stage allocations, captures extras and additional hire, and exports the complete schedule to Excel.

Used by
Commercial teams, quantity surveyors and finance administrators
Starts with
An agreed client rate structure, plot list, extras and hire periods
Produces
A reviewable pricing matrix, stage values, commercial totals and Excel schedule
Try it: Edit a plot value, change a payment-stage percentage, add an extra and export the resulting schedule.This structures agreed pricing; it does not prove completion, issue an invoice or replace contract-specific commercial review.
