What this prototype does

For teams coordinating scaffold inspections: calculate routine deadlines, surface event-triggered checks, capture evidence and keep each defect tied to its corrective action and inspection record.

Used by
Site coordinators, scaffold inspectors and delivery teams
Starts with
Scaffold, trigger, checks and evidence
Produces

Try it: Choose a scenario, review why it is due, inspect the evidence and checks, then record the next human-controlled action.Human control is explicit: the register can surface timing and evidence. A competent person performs the inspection, records the outcome, decides restrictions and signs the report. Completing an action never releases a scaffold automatically.

Inspection timing used in this demonstration

One routine clock, plus event-triggered inspections

Rule basis: HSE scaffold inspection guidance
01

Before first use

Inspect following installation and before a construction scaffold is first used.

02

No more than seven days

Repeat the inspection at an interval of no more than seven days while the scaffold remains in use.

03

After a safety-affecting event

Inspect after circumstances liable to jeopardise safety, such as high winds or a substantial alteration.

04

Competent-person control

A competent person carries out the inspection, records findings and determines the required controls.

More frequent inspection may be required by the risk assessment or competent person. This demonstration supports the record; it is not a substitute for site controls or competent judgement.
